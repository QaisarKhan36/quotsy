Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Quotsy (“the app”) is an invoice and quote maker designed to keep your information private.

Information we collect

None. Quotsy has no user accounts and no servers of its own. The business details, clients, items, invoices and quotes you enter are stored only on your device.

Photos

If you choose to add a logo, the app uses the system photo picker to access only the single image you select. It is resized and stored on your device and used only on your documents.

Sharing

Invoices and quotes leave your device only when you share them yourself, through your device's share menu, to the app and recipient you choose.

Purchases

Optional subscriptions are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store and verified through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details.

Analytics and advertising

Quotsy does not contain advertising and does not use third-party analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase all data in the app, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, nothing remains with us.

Children

Quotsy is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Contact us at the developer email address shown on Quotsy's Google Play listing.
